# Research Direction

## Working Research Question

How can AI-driven, explainable, and interoperable decision intelligence
enhance community digital twins for evidence-based decision support?

## Initial Case Study

Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada.

## Research Context

This research investigates how real-world community data, digital twin
models, predictive analytics, simulation, and AI-driven decision
intelligence can be integrated into a unified decision-support platform.

The research will examine interoperability and transferability between
community digital twin approaches, with particular interest in European
smart-community research and Canadian municipal applications.

## Initial Research Themes

- Community digital twins
- AI-driven decision support
- Explainable AI
- Interoperability
- Predictive analytics and simulation
- Agentic AI
- Human-in-the-loop decision making
- Cross-jurisdictional transferability

## Research Status

This is a preliminary research direction. The research question,
methodology, technologies, and specific contributions will evolve through
literature review, experimentation, doctoral supervision, and evaluation.
