# Community Digital Twin

Research platform for AI-driven, explainable and interoperable decision intelligence over community digital twins.

## Researcher

**Alem Mekru**  
PhD Researcher in Applied Artificial Intelligence  
Alma Mater Europaea University

## Research Focus

The project investigates the integration of real-world community data, digital twins, predictive and simulation models, and AI-driven decision intelligence for explainable, evidence-based decision support.

Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada serves as the initial real-world case study.
